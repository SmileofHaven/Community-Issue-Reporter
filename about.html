Welcome to CivicFix

Got any problems? Just snap it and post!

CivicFix is your direct community platform to report potholes, overflowing drains, broken streetlights, and urban hazards instantly. We bridge the communication gap between citizens and municipal repair crews.

+ Report New Issue View Dashboard

How CivicFix Works

📸
1. Snap & Upload
Take a photo of any civic issue directly from your smartphone camera or gallery.
📍
2. Pin Location
Drop an exact pin on the interactive map so municipal crews can locate the exact spot.
⚡
3. Track to Resolved
Follow ticket statuses live from New to Assigned, In Progress, and Closed.

Empowering Cleaner & Safer Neighbourhoods

Traditional municipal reporting is often slow and opaque. CivicFix brings transparency and accountability to city governance, ensuring every voice and report is heard, tracked, and acted upon efficiently by local authorities.
